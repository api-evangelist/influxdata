Telegraf Enterprise is for teams running Telegraf as critical production infrastructure. It pairs Telegraf Controller — a centralized console for managing Telegraf fleets — with official InfluxData support for both the Controller and the Telegraf agent. If you’re operating anywhere from dozens to tens of thousands of agents, it gives you one place to manage configs, roll out changes safely, and monitor fleet health.
